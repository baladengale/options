- If `pd.action == ACTION_CLOSE` AND `profit_captured >= 50` AND `delta < cfg.profit_take('close_if_delta_above', 0.30)` AND `dte > cfg.profit_take('close_if_dte_below', 21)` → override to HOLD with reason string `"OTM (|Δ|<0.30) with {dte} DTE — hold past 50%, theta still decaying"`
